# Privacy Policy — WordNest

**Last updated: 2 October 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **BinaryIm** ("we", "us", "our") handles information in
the mobile application **WordNest** (the "App"; presented as **Shabdh** in some regions).
The App is a free educational vocabulary game designed for children and families.

We have built the App to be **safe for children** and to collect as little data as
possible. We do **not** ask children for any personal information, and we show
**non-personalized ads only**.

> **Contact:** [SUPPORT EMAIL — e.g. support@yourdomain.com]
> **Developer:** BinaryIm

---

## 1. Our approach to children's privacy

WordNest is directed to children. We comply with:

- the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** in the United States,
- the **EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation**, including protections for children
  (**GDPR-K**), and
- India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP)**.

Because the App is child-directed, all advertising is served in **non-personalized
(contextual)** mode, and we do **not** request consent from children for personalized
advertising or any form of tracking.

## 2. Information we do NOT collect

We do **not** collect, ask for, or require any of the following:

- Name, email address, phone number, or postal address
- Account registration or login of any kind
- Precise (GPS) location
- Photos, camera, microphone, contacts, or files
- Any information typed by the child

The App has **no chat, no social features, and no user-generated content sharing.**

## 3. Information collected automatically by third-party services

To keep the App running, understand how it performs, and show age-appropriate ads, we
use the trusted third-party services below. These services may automatically collect
limited technical information such as **device type and operating system, app version,
non-identifying/"pseudonymous" device identifiers, approximate region (derived from
device settings or IP address), in-app events (e.g. levels played), and crash
diagnostics.** This data is used only for the purposes described and is handled in a
child-directed / non-personalized manner.

| Service | Provider | Purpose | Privacy Policy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Firebase Analytics | Google LLC | Understand aggregate, anonymous app usage | https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy |
| Google Firebase Crashlytics | Google LLC | Diagnose crashes and stability | https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy |
| Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation | Unity Technologies | Deliver non-personalized ads | https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy |
| Google AdMob | Google LLC | Non-personalized ad delivery | https://policies.google.com/privacy |
| AppLovin | AppLovin Corp. | Non-personalized ad delivery | https://www.applovin.com/privacy/ |
| Unity Ads | Unity Technologies | Non-personalized ad delivery | https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy |

Ad providers may process a device identifier and IP address solely to **deliver ads,
cap ad frequency, and prevent fraud** — never to build advertising profiles of children.

## 4. Advertising

The App shows **non-personalized ads only**. These ads are based on general, contextual
signals (such as the content of the App), **not** on a user's past behavior or interests.
We instruct every ad network to treat all users as subject to child-directed treatment
(COPPA) and to serve non-personalized ads (GDPR). We participate in Google Play's
**Designed for Families** program, and ad content is restricted to be age-appropriate.

## 5. Information stored on the device

Your game progress (such as levels completed and settings) is stored **locally on the
device** and is not transmitted to us. Removing the App deletes this local data.

## 6. How we use information

The limited technical information above is used only to:

- operate and maintain the App,
- measure aggregate, anonymous usage and improve the App,
- diagnose crashes and fix bugs, and
- deliver non-personalized, age-appropriate advertising.

We do **not** sell personal information, and we do **not** use data to build profiles of
children or to serve personalized/behavioral advertising.

## 7. Data sharing

We do not share information with anyone except the service providers listed in Section 3,
who process it only to provide their service to us under their own privacy terms. We may
disclose information if required by law.

## 8. Data retention

We retain the aggregate analytics and crash data for only as long as needed for the
purposes above, after which it is deleted or anonymized in line with each provider's
retention policies.

## 9. Security

We rely on reputable providers (Google, Unity, AppLovin) whose services transmit data
over **encrypted (HTTPS/TLS)** connections. No method of transmission is 100% secure, but
we take reasonable measures to protect information.

## 10. International data transfers

Our service providers may process data on servers located outside your country. These
providers maintain safeguards for international transfers as described in their policies.

## 11. Parental rights and choices

Parents and guardians may:

- **Review or request deletion** of any data associated with their child by contacting us
  at the email above (please describe the device so we can assist the providers in
  locating relevant records).
- **Opt out of ads measurement** at the OS level ("Opt out of Ads Personalization" /
  resetting the advertising ID on Android, or "Limit Ad Tracking" on iOS).
- **Stop all collection** by uninstalling the App.

We will respond to verified parental requests promptly and will not condition a child's
use of the App on providing more information than is reasonably necessary.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated"
date above and, for material changes, provide a prominent notice within the App or store
listing.
